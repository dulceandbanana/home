Your order, your information.

This page explains how the Dulce & Banana website uses information entered into an order request. It should be reviewed by the business owner before the website is published.

What we collect

If you send a preorder through the connected checkout, we collect your name, mobile number, optional email, preferred fulfillment date, delivery or pickup area, selected products, and any notes you choose to share. This information is needed to review and fulfill the request. Please avoid putting sensitive personal information in the notes.

How orders are handled

When online ordering is connected, the order is stored in the bakery's private order-management database and can be accessed by approved bakery administrators. If you use the Facebook ordering option, information is handled through Facebook/Messenger subject to that provider's terms and privacy practices.

Courier quotations and location pins

For delivery quotations, customers may choose a precise destination pin on a map or allow location access on their device. The delivery address and selected coordinates are sent to our courier quotation provider, Lalamove, to request a route-based estimate. We do not request your device location unless you select the location button. Location data associated with submitted orders is kept in the bakery's restricted order system; quotes are temporary and may expire. Please provide the recipient's consent before entering another person's location.

Payments

This website does not currently collect payment card details. After approval of the final amount, customers may use GCash, Maya or MariBank transfer QR codes. A transaction reference reported through this website is treated as awaiting verification; staff must confirm receipt in the receiving account. We store the transfer method, claimed amount and transfer reference for this verification.

Website preferences

The website uses local device storage to remember your display theme and basket. Personal checkout details are not stored in the browser's local storage.

Your questions and requests

For questions about a request, updates, corrections, or deletion of information, contact Dulce & Banana through its Facebook page. Any legal, tax, retention, and consumer-notice obligations should be finalized by the business owner before launch.
