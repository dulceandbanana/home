Our story

Good things are made at home.

Dulce & Banana is a home-based bakery in Las Piñas, where every order begins in a family kitchen and is made with attention and care.

From muffins and banana loaves to cookies for sharing, we believe the smallest treats can make someone's day a little sweeter.

We're currently accepting orders by arrangement; we're not a walk-in café.

Good to know

Before the first bite.

Homemade bakes, ordered with care. Need something specific? Message us before placing your request.

Do you have a physical café?

Not yet. Dulce & Banana is a home-based bakery in Las Piñas, preparing orders by arrangement.

When will my order be ready?

Choose a preferred date at checkout. We will confirm the baking schedule and availability before your order is accepted.

How much is delivery?

Delivery pricing uses a live Lalamove estimate based on a delivery pin, measured from Velmont Residences, Las Piñas, when Lalamove is configured. Delivery is quoted again at fulfillment and the confirmed amount may change.

Can I request allergen information?

Yes. Please contact us for ingredients, allergen handling, and storage information before placing an order. We cannot guarantee an allergen-free kitchen.

Can I personalize a gift?

Add ribbons and a card for ₱10, and include the gift note in your checkout details.
